Support

Need help with Ringlate? Write to yazilim@cw-enerji.com. Please include your device model, operating system version, the app version (Settings → About) and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

How do I delete my account? Settings → Privacy and Security → Delete account. This removes your profile and your content from our servers.

How do I sign out of another device? Settings → Devices lists every active session; you can end any of them.

Are my messages end-to-end encrypted? Messages are encrypted in transit and stored on our servers so they are available on all your devices; they are not end-to-end encrypted. See the Privacy Policy.

I lost access to my e-mail. Contact us from any other address with details that let us verify you are the account owner.
